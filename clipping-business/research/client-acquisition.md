# Client Acquisition (Future: Clipping Agency)

## The Spec-Clip Framework
Don't pitch. Deliver value first.

1. Find a creator with unoptimized content (horizontal clips, no captions, bad framing)
2. Download 30 min of their content
3. Edit 2 polished vertical clips for free
4. Send them with a short email
5. Ask for a 10-min call

## Cold Email Template
```
Subject: 2 Spec-Clips for [Creator Name] - Captured From Thursday's Stream

Hi [First Name],

I noticed your long-form broadcasts are driving great engagement, 
but your TikTok clips are posted as raw horizontal VODs. 
Vertical algorithms heavily penalize this — it's likely limiting your reach.

I edited 2 high-retention spec-clips from last Thursday's stream:
- Optimized 9:16 layout
- Burned-in animated captions
- Reframed for mobile

Download both watermark-free here: [LINK]

If you like them, would you be open to a 10-min call Thursday 
to discuss packaging your weekly content into 15 short-form assets/week?

Best,
[Name]
```

## Follow-Up Protocol
- Max 3 follow-up emails
- Keep brief and outcome-focused
- Reference their specific content in each follow-up

## Where to Find Clients
- Twitch streamers with no vertical clip presence
- Podcasters with YouTube but no Shorts
- Business coaches with long YouTube videos, no TikTok
- Local businesses with no short-form presence

## Pricing Benchmarks
- Entry retainer: $500-$1,500/mo (5-10 clips/week)
- Mid-tier: $2,000-$5,000/mo (15-20 clips/week)
- Agency scale: $5,000+/mo (daily posting, multi-platform)

## Note
This is a future revenue stream. Focus on Whop campaigns first to build skills and proof.
