# Niche Research

## High-Converting Niches for Clipping (ranked by save rate + CPM)

### 1. Finance / Make Money Online
- Why it works: Specific dollar amounts stop scrollers
- Best hook format: "I made $X doing Y"
- Top creators to clip: Russell Brunson, Alex Hormozi, Iman Gadzhi, Codie Sanchez
- Affiliate potential: High (trading communities, courses)
- Seasonality: Year-round, peaks Jan (New Year resolutions) and Sep (back to hustle)

### 2. Sports Picks / Betting
- Why it works: Urgency (tonight's game), social proof (win streaks)
- Best hook format: "Went 5-1 last week. Here's tonight's lock."
- Seasonality: NFL (Sep-Feb), NBA (Oct-Jun), UFC (year-round), World Cup (summers)
- Affiliate potential: Very high (Whop sports picks communities)
- Note: World Cup 2026 is happening NOW — peak season

### 3. Comedy / Entertainment
- Why it works: Easy to clip, high share rate
- Best hook format: Reaction / unexpected punchline
- Top creators: Bert Kreischer, Ali Siddiq, Felipe Esparza
- Seasonality: Year-round
- Note: Low CPM campaigns but easy volume

### 4. Fitness / Health
- Why it works: Transformation + shame/aspiration triggers saves
- Best hook format: "I did X for 30 days and this happened"
- Seasonality: Peaks Jan, May (summer body), Sep
- Note: Pepty SLIDESHOWS [HEALTH] campaign available at $3/1K

### 5. Trading / Crypto
- Why it works: Money + status + FOMO
- Best hook format: "Made $X on this trade"
- Top campaigns: ColezTrades ($1/1K $14K budget), FSP Warriors ($3/1K)
- Seasonality: Year-round, peaks during market volatility
