# Niche Research

## Niche Tier Matrix (ranked by CPM/RPM)

### Tier 1 — Premium ($25-$65 CPM)
- Personal Finance, Real Estate, Tax, Legal, Wealth Building
- 3-5 posts/week
- Monetization: Digital products, high-ticket affiliates, SaaS
- Advertisers: Banks, brokerages, fintech, tax software
- **Russell Brunson falls here** — marketing/funnels = high CPM

### Tier 2 — High ($12-$25 CPM)
- B2B SaaS, Software Tutorials, Career, Health Education
- 3-5 posts/week
- Monetization: Recurring software affiliates, course sales, sponsorships

### Tier 3 — Moderate ($6-$12 CPM)
- Cooking, Fitness, History, Travel
- 5-7 posts/week
- Monetization: Creator rewards, low-ticket planners, ecom affiliates

### Tier 4 — Low ($2-$8 CPM)
- General entertainment, gaming, reaction, trending topics
- 7-14 posts/week
- Monetization: Programmatic ads, live gifting
- **Avoid unless you have massive volume**

## Niches to Focus On (our operation)

### 1. Finance / Make Money Online (PRIMARY)
- Why: Tier 1 CPM ($25-65), Russell Brunson content available
- Best hook: "I made $X doing Y"
- Top creators: Russell Brunson, Alex Hormozi, Iman Gadzhi, Codie Sanchez
- Seasonality: Year-round, peaks Jan + Sep
- Campaign: Russell Brunson $3/1K on Whop

### 2. Sports Picks / Betting
- Why: High conversion rates (15-20%) on Whop affiliate offers
- Best hook: "Went 5-1 last week. Here's tonight's lock."
- Seasonality: NFL (Sep-Feb), NBA (Oct-Jun), UFC (year-round), World Cup NOW
- Campaign: Whop affiliate (Ghostsportzpickz, Lev's Locks)

### 3. Fitness / Health
- Why: Pepty SLIDESHOWS campaign at $3/1K available
- Best hook: "I did X for 30 days and this happened"
- Seasonality: Jan, May, Sep
