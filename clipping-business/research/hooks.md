# Hook Performance Data

## What the Data Shows (from $37M YouTube revenue study)

### Title/Hook Formats Ranked by Revenue per Click
1. **First person + dollar amount as rate** — "I Make $8,300/Month From..." → 5x revenue vs other formats
2. **First person outcome** — "How I..." → 52% of all tracked revenue, 37% of clicks
3. **Generic educational** — "How to..." → only 18% of revenue despite 32% of clicks

### Rules for Slide 1 (Hook)
- Under 8 words
- Include a specific number if possible
- First person beats third person
- Specific beats vague ("$8,300/month" beats "make money")
- Urgency beats evergreen ("tonight" beats "every week")

### Save Rate Benchmarks
- Above 5% save rate = strong purchase intent, scale immediately
- 2-5% save rate = decent, tweak CTA
- Under 1% = awareness only, won't convert

### Best Performing Hook Templates
- "I made $[amount] doing [simple thing]"
- "[Number] things I stopped doing that made me $[amount]"
- "Went [W-L record] last week. Tonight's lock:"
- "This [thing] makes me $[amount]/month on autopilot"
- "I almost deleted this. It now makes me $[amount]"
