# Platform Best Practices

## TikTok
- Best post times: 9am, 12pm, 6pm local
- Carousel saves = strongest conversion signal
- Hook must land in first 1.5 seconds
- Hashtags at end of caption, not beginning
- New account: expect 200-500 views per post for first 3-5 posts
- Breaking 1,000 views on a new account = algorithm approved, iterate that format

## Instagram Reels
- Carousels get pushed harder than single images
- Save rate is tracked and affects distribution
- Best times: 8am, 11am, 7pm
- Use 3-5 hashtags max

## YouTube Shorts
- Longer watch time = more distribution
- First frame must be scroll-stopping
- Good for repurposing winning TikTok content

## Posting Strategy
- Start with TikTok only
- When a format wins on TikTok, repost to Instagram and Shorts
- Don't spread thin across all platforms at once
- 2-3 posts per day minimum to get algorithm signal
